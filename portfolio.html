The Harrington Estate

A multi-year restoration project of a historic 18th-century country estate. The brief was to reinstate the lost formal parterres near the house while transitioning into wild, endemic meadows as the property met the surrounding woodland.

We designed a bespoke Yorkstone terrace that steps gracefully down to a newly engineered lake, creating a seamless visual flow from the classical architecture to the organic landscape.

Nexus Tech Campus

Our largest public realm project to date. Nexus required an outdoor environment that actively promoted employee wellness, collaboration, and biodiversity within a dense urban context.

The solution was a highly structured grid of sunken courtyards, connected by elevated walkways through mature pleached trees. A state-of-the-art smart irrigation system reuses 100% of the site's rainwater runoff.

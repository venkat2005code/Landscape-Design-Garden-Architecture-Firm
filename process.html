Phase 01

Discovery & Consultation

We begin by understanding you, your property, and how you intend to use the space. This is a crucial phase of listening and site analysis.

Our Deliverables

  • Initial on-site or virtual consultation
  • Topographical and boundary survey
  • Soil sampling and microclimate analysis
  • Project scope and initial fee proposal
Timeline: 2 - 3 Weeks

What to Prepare

  • Any existing house plans or surveys
  • Pinterest boards or inspirational imagery
  • A list of desired functions (e.g., dining, play, secluded reading)
  • Understanding of your ideal budget range
Phase 02 & 03

Design Development

Concept Presentation

We present mood boards, initial sketches, and spatial layouts. This is a collaborative review to ensure our vision aligns perfectly with yours before technical work begins.

Masterplan & 3D

Refining the concept into a rigid masterplan. We provide 3D renders of key sightlines, allowing you to walk through the space virtually before ground is broken.

Technical Detailing

Creating exact CAD drawings for hardscaping, bespoke joinery, and lighting schematics. This ensures precise pricing and flawless execution by the build team.

Phase 04

Build & Installation

Where drawings become reality. Our in-house management ensures every stone is laid and every tree planted exactly to specification.

We manage the entire tendering and build process. From groundworks and drainage to the final placement of furniture, you have a single point of contact holding the contractors to our exacting standards.

Phase 05

Seasonal Aftercare

A garden is never truly 'finished'. We offer ongoing horticultural maintenance to ensure the landscape matures exactly as intended.

Spring

Pruning of deciduous shrubs, application of organic mulches, and checking irrigation lines before the growing season.

Summer

Deadheading for continuous blooms, monitoring water systems during dry spells, and structural hedging trims.

Autumn

Planting of spring bulbs, lifting of tender perennials, and structural pruning to ensure winter silhouettes.

Winter

Hardwood pruning, servicing of water features, and assessing soil health for the coming year.
